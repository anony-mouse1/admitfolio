# School logo provenance

These files are served only from `/assets/schools/`. University names and marks
remain the property of their respective owners. This inventory records where
each production asset came from and does not claim university endorsement.

## Existing Admitfolio WebP assets

The following files were copied byte-for-byte from
`public/mockup-assets/university-logos/` into the production asset namespace.
The repository does not record their original upstream URLs. Most were added by
commit `e10b01a89a00eea0d6d3525069c58ed8a14570a4` on 2026-08-19. Duke,
Florida, Indiana, and Washington were added by commit
`21d3ba610d917039833ab31a3d9185aa8a46b8ff` on 2026-08-20. The UChicago seal
was supplied directly by the project owner and introduced by commit
`69d704e54fe055e1799c5a97642e9c598c5f0dfc` on 2026-08-19.
